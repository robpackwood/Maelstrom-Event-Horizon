# Audio sources

All newly added or enabled audio is commercially usable CC0/public-domain material.

## Sound effects

- **60 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX** by rubberduck, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/60-cc0-sci-fi-sfx
- The supplied OGGs were converted to WAV for reliable WPF playback. Curated clips are used for player/enemy fire, hazard warnings, black holes, novas, power-ups, extra ships, cash celebrations, comets, and boss alerts.
